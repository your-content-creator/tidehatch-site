Last updated 23 November 2026

1.The short version

You can get a full refund on your first payment of a plan (Creator, Founder or Team) or a top-up within 14 days, if you used fewer than 10 credits from that payment.

2.The main rule

  • It covers the first payment of a plan or of a top-up.
  • You must ask within 14 days of paying.
  • You must have used fewer than 10 of the credits from that payment.
  • If all three are true, you get your money back in full.

3.What is not refunded

Otherwise, we do not refund credits you have used or partial months. This does not change any rights you have under the law where you live.

4.How credits work with refunds

  • Credits are held when a step starts and taken only if the step works. Failed steps cost nothing, so you do not need a refund for them.
  • When a payment is refunded, or you open a chargeback, we take back the matching credits. If the chargeback is decided in our favor, the credits come back. Your balance can go below zero. Paid steps are blocked until it is fixed.

5.The earned first-month offer

If you finish your first script on the free plan, we may email you an offer: 50% off your first Creator month, valid 7 days, Creator only, once. If you used it, a refund under this policy is the amount you actually paid.

6.Cancelling

You can cancel any time: open Settings and tap Manage or cancel plan. Your plan then stops at the end of the month you paid for. Cancelling stops the next payment. It does not refund the current month unless the main rule above applies.

7.If you live in the EU or the UK

In the EU and the UK, you normally have 14 days to change your mind about an online purchase. For Tidehatch:

  • At checkout, you ask us to start the service right away.
  • At checkout, you also agree that your 14-day right to withdraw ends once you use credits.
  • Our refund rule above still applies to you as described, and your other legal rights stay in place.

8.How to ask for a refund

Email contact@mjcreativellc.com from the email address on your Tidehatch account, and tell us which payment you mean. We will reply, and if you qualify, the money goes back to the card you paid with through Stripe.
