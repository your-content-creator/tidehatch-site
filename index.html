Features

Everything you need to make short-form videos that win

Behind Tidehatch is a team of AI agents. Each one does one job well, and they hand work to each other so you don't start from a blank page.

Research agent

Find the winning channels in your niche

Track the channels you compete with on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn, in one feed.

Outlier agent

See which videos beat their own channel's normal views by 5x, 10x, 50x. That's where the real ideas are.

Why-it-won agent

FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is Tidehatch for?

Founders, creators and agencies who use short-form video to grow a business, and who want to know what to post before they film it.

Which platforms does it cover?

Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn for research and analytics, plus YouTube long-form ideas and titles.

Do I have to connect my social accounts?

No. Research and scripts work without it. Connecting your accounts adds your own analytics, so ideas and advice are built on your numbers.

Will the scripts sound like me?

Yes. Tidehatch learns your voice from your own videos and posts, and every script comes in two versions so you can test which one your audience likes.

Who is behind Tidehatch?

MJ Creative, a short-form content agency. Tidehatch is the research and script system we built for our own clients, now opening up to everyone.

When does Tidehatch launch?

The public launch date is to be announced. It's in private beta with our agency clients now, and the early access list gets in first.

What do I get for joining early?

The first 100 people get a free 7 Video Ideas report for their niche. Everyone on the list gets founding pricing and first access.

Does joining the list cost anything?

No. Joining is free and doesn't commit you to buy. We'll email you when early access opens.

How much will it cost?

Prices come out at launch, and the list sees them first. There will be three plans: Starter, Pro and Team.

What do you do with my email?

We only use it to send Tidehatch launch news and your bonus. We never sell it, and you can unsubscribe anytime.

What is the Hatch?

Your private vault. Every idea you save, every script you write and every finished video lives there, so nothing gets lost in notes apps and chats.

Can I organize ideas into collections?

Yes. Group ideas by topic, series, platform or client, and move them from idea to script to filmed to posted.

Can I save a video from someone else's channel?

Yes. Paste a link from Instagram, TikTok or YouTube and Tidehatch saves it to your collection with its views and a why-it-won breakdown.

Who can see my collections?

Only you, and anyone you invite. On the Team plan, each client gets their own private space.
