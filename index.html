FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is Tidehatch for?

Founders, creators and small brands who post short-form video to grow a business, and want to know what to film before they film it.

Which platforms does it work for?

YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and Facebook Reels. Tidehatch reads the videos on all four, finds the outliers and writes scripts you can film for any of them. Topic search runs on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. Tidehatch doesn’t post or schedule for you.

Can Tidehatch promise views?

No. The screenshots on this page are one client’s best results. Most videos get far less. Tidehatch shows you what already won in your niche, so you start from proof instead of a guess.

Do I need to connect my accounts?

No. Paste your public profile links and Tidehatch reads your videos. Nothing to log in to, nothing to connect.

Can I use Tidehatch from my own AI?

Yes, on Founder and Team. Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor or any assistant that supports MCP. It reads your outliers, your persona and your scripts, and can run steps for you, from finding creators to writing Script A. Steps use the same credits as the app.

Will the scripts sound like me?

Tidehatch writes your persona from your own videos and links, and you can change anything in it. Scripts follow it. Each idea gets Script A and Script B, so you can test which one your audience likes.

Do I need a card to try it?

No. Free needs only your email. When the 50 free credits run out, nothing is charged. You pick a paid plan only if you want one.

What is a credit?

One credit pays for one step, like qualifying an idea. Bigger steps cost more: a script is 3, a research brief is 10. Editing by hand is free, and a step that fails costs nothing.

Who can see my ideas and scripts?

You, and anyone you add to your workspace. To run a step, Tidehatch sends what it needs to its AI and data providers (listed in the Privacy Policy). Your data is never sold.

Do you use Tidehatch yourself?

Yes. I built Tidehatch for my own short-form work, and I use it every week. Each week I check what the videos did, and fix what Tidehatch got wrong.

Who is behind Tidehatch?

I'm Marko. I run a short-form video studio, and I built Tidehatch to find ideas and write scripts for the people I work with. Now you can use it too.
