2.What Tidehatch does

Tidehatch is an AI tool for short-form video creators. It finds outlier videos, shows why they won, and writes research briefs, hooks and scripts. You pay with credits.

AI output can be wrong, out of date, or too much like something else. Check it before you use it. You are the one who decides what to film and post.

6.Credits and plans

The full details are on the site and in the app. In short:

  • Free: 50 credits once, no card. Daily limit of 25 credits. The daily Watchlist feed checks 1 of your creators each morning.
  • Creator plan: $29 a month for 150 credits. Unused plan credits roll over for one month, then expire. Includes the daily Watchlist feed: every morning Tidehatch checks 5 of your creators, in turn.
  • Founder plan: $59 a month for 400 credits. Everything in Creator, plus up to 5 workspaces (one per brand) and a Watchlist of up to 50 creators. Unused plan credits roll over for one month, then expire. The daily Watchlist feed checks 10 of your creators each morning, shared across your workspaces. Includes Connect your own AI (MCP).
  • Team plan: $149 a month for 1,000 credits. Up to 15 workspaces and 5 seats (you plus 4 editors) on one shared credit pool, paid by the owner. The daily Watchlist feed checks 20 of your creators each morning, shared across your workspaces. Unused plan credits roll over for one month, then expire. Includes Connect your own AI (MCP).
  • Top-up: 50 credits for $12, on any plan. Top-up credits never expire.
  • Welcome offer: a welcome offer we may email you after sign-up: 50% off your first Creator month, valid 14 days, Creator only, once. It is not shown on the site and not every account gets it.
  • Held, then taken: credits are held when a step starts and are taken only when the step works. Failed steps are free.
  • Pause after failures: after 5 failed steps in one day, the account pauses until the next day.
  • Revisions: free, up to 30 a day on Creator and Founder and 10 a day on Free, while you still have credits.

Prices are in US dollars, plus VAT or sales tax where it applies. Stripe Tax works out the amount at checkout.

7.Payments, cancelling and refunds

Payments are handled by Stripe. Pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or the other methods shown at checkout. Credits from a bank payment arrive once the payment clears. Your statement shows MJC TIDEHATCH (top-ups may show MJ CREATIVE* TIDEHATCH).

Auto-renewal. Creator, Founder and Team renew automatically every month at the plan price until you cancel. You can cancel any time: open Settings and tap Manage or cancel plan. Your plan then stops at the end of the month you already paid for, and you are not charged again.

Refunds follow our Refund Policy. If a payment is refunded or you open a chargeback, we take back the matching credits. If the chargeback is decided in our favor, we give those credits back. Your balance can go below zero. While it is below zero, paid steps are blocked until it is fixed.
