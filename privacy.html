1.Who we are

Tidehatch (tidehatch.com, and the app at app.tidehatch.com) is run by MJ Creative LLC, 75 East 3rd Street, Ste 7, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA. In this page, “we” and “us” mean MJ Creative LLC. If you have any question about your data, write to contact@mjcreativellc.com.

2.What this page is for

This page says what information we keep about you, why we keep it, who helps us handle it, and what you can ask us to do. We try to say it in plain words.

Short version: we keep what we need to run your account. We do not sell your personal data. We do not store the videos you analyze.

3.What we keep

We keep these things:

  • Account email and sign-in data. So you can log in.
  • Your workspace content. Your ideas, briefs, scripts, notes, and your Watchlist of public creator accounts.
  • Credit and payment records. What you bought, what credits you have, and what you used. Your card details stay with Stripe. We never see or keep your full card number.
  • Usage logs. Simple records of what happens in the app, so we can fix bugs, stop abuse and count credits.
  • Trial check. A one-way hash of your email: a scrambled code that cannot be turned back into your email. It lets us give each person only one free trial. We keep it even after you delete your account.

4.Public videos and what we keep from them

When you give us a link to a public video, we look at it and write an analysis. We keep only our analysis, which is text. We never store the video itself or its frames.

Tidehatch also collects public post data (like titles, captions and view counts) from the public accounts on your Watchlist. A daily job deletes this data 30 days after we collect it. If you save a post as an idea, the idea keeps the title, link and the numbers you saved, until you delete the idea or your account.

5.YouTube data

Some features use YouTube API Services. If you use them, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google handles data under the Google Privacy Policy.

6.Why we use your data

For each use, this is the legal reason (the “lawful basis”) that lets us do it:

  • To give you the service: sign you in, run your steps, keep your workspace, and count credits. Basis: contract (we need it to give you what you signed up for).
  • To take payments and keep payment records. Basis: contract, and the tax and accounting rules we must follow.
  • To email you about your account (sign-in links and receipts). Basis: contract.
  • To email you about your free trial: at most a reminder to finish your first script, one offer for your first paid month and one reminder before it ends, and once after 25 days on a paid plan. Basis: legitimate interest (telling you about the plan you are trying). Reply STOP to any of these and the offer emails stop within 10 business days.
  • To send Tidehatch news, only if you subscribed to the newsletter. Basis: consent. You can unsubscribe any time.
  • To keep the service safe and stop abuse, like people making many free trials. Basis: legitimate interest.
  • To make the product better, using simple visit counts and usage logs. Basis: legitimate interest.

8.We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal data. We do not rent it out either.

9.Cookies

We only use the cookies or similar storage that signing in needs. Our site visit counter (GoatCounter) does not use cookies. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when a page opens.

10.How long we keep data

  • Raw public post data: deleted by a daily job 30 days after we collect it. Ideas you save keep the title, link and the numbers you saved.
  • Trial check: the one-way hash of your email is kept after you delete your account, so a new free trial cannot be claimed with the same email.
  • Your account and workspace: kept while your account is open. You can ask us to delete it at any time (see section 11).
  • Payment and credit records: we may need to keep these for as long as tax and accounting rules say.

11.Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Access: tell you what we hold about you.
  • Export: give you a copy of it.
  • Correct: fix anything that is wrong.
  • Delete: remove your data.
  • Object: stop us using your data in a way you do not agree with.
  • Withdraw consent: unsubscribe from the newsletter any time.

To ask, email contact@mjcreativellc.com from your account email. A delete-my-data request also removes your email from our newsletter list (Kit) and our email sender (Resend), and closes your Stripe customer record. We keep only the one-way hash of your email (see section 3) and the payment records the law asks us to keep.

If you live in the EU or the UK and you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

12.Age limit

You must be at least 16 years old to use Tidehatch. If you think a child under 16 has signed up, tell us and we will close the account.

13.Changes to this page

If we change this page in a way that matters, we will email you before it starts. The date at the top always shows the latest version.
